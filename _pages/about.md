## About

I am a first-year PhD candidate in the [HKUST NLP Group](https://www.google.com/search?q=HKUST+NLP+Group), led by [Professor Junxian He](https://www.google.com/search?q=Junxian+He), at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with interests in LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models, and LLM truthfulness and interpretability.

## Academic Background
